# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-11)

- First release.
- Viewer for X12 and EDIFACT files: tree of interchanges, groups, transaction sets or messages, loops (HL hierarchy, NM1, N1, PO1, LIN and more) and segments, with element names and code meanings.
- Segments table with a filter, and a Problems tab.
- Checks: ISA field widths, control numbers and counts in every envelope, segments that do not belong to the transaction set, empty mandatory elements.
- Text editor: colours, element names on hover, problems in the Problems panel, Format EDI and Minify EDI.
- Zovo Pro tools, 3 free tries each: export to JSON or CSV, compare two EDI files, validate a folder.
- The walkthrough ends with a step that links free Zovo extensions for files that often come with EDI files. A link opens the extension page; nothing installs on its own.
- Small links at the bottom of the viewer: Rate, More free viewers, Zovo Pro (USD 19 once, for every Zovo extension), PayPal and Hide.
